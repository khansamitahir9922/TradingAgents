## Goal
Provide split-adjusted price returns and separately labelled total returns. Fix a prediction's target and horizon explicitly, and prove its outcome was complete and available before it can enter a training fold. These are research results, not ledger P&L.

## Contracts
```python
class ReturnBasis(StrEnum):
    SPLIT_ADJUSTED_PRICE_RETURN = "SPLIT_ADJUSTED_PRICE_RETURN"
    TOTAL_RETURN_CLOSE_REINVESTED = "TOTAL_RETURN_CLOSE_REINVESTED"

@dataclass(frozen=True)
class ReturnObservation:
    source: str; instrument_id: str
    start_session: date; end_session: date; basis: ReturnBasis
    value: float | None
    outcome_end: datetime; available_from: datetime; as_of: datetime
    bar_refs: tuple[BarRef, ...]; action_refs: tuple[ActionRef, ...]
    coverage: ActionCoverage; unavailable_reason: str

@dataclass(frozen=True)
class LabelConfig:
    horizon_sessions: int           # provisional baseline 5, never inferred from a model
    basis: ReturnBasis
    version: str

def session_returns(reader, source, instrument_id, first, last, *, at, calendar,
                    coverage, basis) -> tuple[ReturnObservation, ...]
def outcome_at(reader, source, instrument_id, prediction_session, *,
               config: LabelConfig, at, calendar, coverage) -> ReturnObservation
def training_eligible(outcome: ReturnObservation, training_cutoff: datetime) -> bool
```

## Rules
1. All reads are explicitly PIT at `at`, per provider. Bar and action identities, currency, raw bases and coverage are checked. Future effective actions and revisions unavailable at `at` are excluded. Split-adjusted price return uses the two prices on one split basis; missing sessions give unavailable observations, never compressed time or zero returns.
2. Total return is a separate close-to-close index, not adjusted OHLC. For each adjacent session, start with one share at the preceding raw close. Apply effective splits to its quantity; a same-date dividend is the stored as-traded amount per post-split share (validate this convention against the verified import semantics). Gross return is `(ending_quantity * current_raw_close + dividend_cash) / preceding_raw_close`. Reinvest dividend cash at that session's close and chain daily gross returns. No dividend means zero cash only when dividend coverage is established. Split-only return is zero for prices 100 then 50 and ratio 2; adding a dividend of 1 per new share gives 2% total return. Unknown action conventions raise `AdjustmentUnavailable` rather than guessing.
3. Total-return coverage includes SPLIT and DIVIDEND and the previous close needed at the first ex-date. A missing previous close, insufficient coverage, currency mismatch, non-positive split ratio or non-finite result prevents calculation. Exact split/dividend/share arithmetic stays Decimal until the explicit float64 boundary. Stored prices and actions remain byte-for-byte unchanged.
4. An h-session outcome ends at the calendar close of the h-th session after the prediction session. `outcome_end` and all used inputs' `available_from` must be <= training cutoff before eligibility is true. An end-of-period timestamp alone does not prove that a late-observed label was available. Recompute labels at that fold's cutoff, never read today's final revisions into past folds.
5. The five-session horizon and target basis are named configuration, provisional under OPEN-09. Direction evaluation initially uses split-adjusted price return; a total-return result is reported separately and cannot silently replace that target.

## Implementation choices
Resolved: close reinvestment is the explicit total-return convention; it does not model broker fills, tax, financing, fees or the future ledger. Unsupported corporate-action types make the affected outcome unavailable. Universe scope is fixed to NVDA, not selected after inspecting results.

Unresolved: independent review of same-date action handling, horizon and return convention before acceptance. No external data is needed to write synthetic acceptance tests.

## Acceptance tests
- Synthetic split, dividend, simultaneous split/dividend and multi-day chained examples, hand-calculated; clearly distinct price and total returns.
- Future split and late dividend correction excluded at earlier T; provider separation; full revision references and BACKFILL_ASSUMED labels preserved.
- Missing internal session/previous close and unestablished dividend coverage return an explicit unavailable reason, never zero.
- Labels ending after training cutoff, observed after cutoff, or corrected after cutoff cannot leak into that training fold. Holiday/half-day horizon uses sessions, not elapsed weekdays.
- Stored file hashes unchanged; invalid/non-finite inputs refused.

## Out of scope
Numeric-return forecasting (M9), intraday labels, ledger entries, taxes and real execution.
