## Contracts

The [M5 shared contracts](M5-README.md#shared-contracts) and accepted ADR-010 govern this task. Proposed rules for this task:

1. Implement FIFO ordering and lot consumption; preserve acquisition fill/opening-lot reference, original and remaining quantities, total cost and policy version.
2. Use ADR-010 v0.2 cumulative half-even allocation: target cumulative cost is exact C*d/Q rounded at 0.0001 and bounded by prior allocation and total C; this match gets target minus prior allocation. Full disposal uses exact C. Use exact rational tie comparison. Never independently round C/Q for each partial fill. Splits start a new remaining-cost/quantity allocation epoch in the following task.
3. Fees are expensed once at their fill time; gross realized gains exclude fees. Never infer cost from a mark, average execution price of another order, or current inventory value.
4. A sale consuming unavailable/insufficient lots is AccountingUnavailable, not a short position or a guessed basis.

## Acceptance tests

- Two BUY lots and a SELL across both consume the older lot first; equal-time acquisitions use stable IDs.
- Successive partial sells conserve cost, including recurring allocations and final residual; six one-share disposals of a 0.0004-cost lot allocate [0.0001,0,0.0001,0.0001,0,0.0001], never a negative remainder. Grouped disposals give the same cumulative cost.
- Profit, loss, zero-profit and fee-exceeds-proceeds journal cases balance with nonnegative entry sides.
- Changed global Decimal precision, rounding and traps do not change results or IDs; invalid enormous/unrepresentable operations fail explicitly.
