## Contracts

The [M5 shared contracts](M5-README.md#shared-contracts) and accepted ADR-010 govern this task. Proposed rules for this task:

1. Implement balances(E,K) and sync_account in C2, with all entries, action revisions, source receipts and lot matches restricted by the selected book head and cutoffs.
2. Compute affected dependency closure for late fills and revised actions, including downstream FIFO sales and dividends. Reverse old affected transactions in their original effective times, append replacements posted at the newly observed processing time, commit once.
3. Unchanged source set is a no-op. Same source identity with differing immutable content is a conflict. Complete closure must be proven; missing prerequisites cannot advance the successful book head.
4. Net P&L identity and unmarked-position status follow C3. Reports do not use a price unavailable at K or a pre-action raw mark for post-action shares.
5. Never discard/rebuild the ledger to make a broker mismatch disappear. Recalculation produces proposed append-only corrections only from validated new source evidence.

## Acceptance tests

- A pre-split fill delivered after a later sale changes only queries at/after its posting knowledge; FIFO realized cost and any affected dividend also correct.
- Changing a split ratio after a partial sale reverses/replaces every dependent allocation, preserving exact total cash/cost/quantities.
- Earlier K queries are byte-identical before and after correction; equal-timestamp arrivals preserve the old pinned book head; effective-time-only filtering is caught by a regression.
- Two successive corrections reverse the active replacement each time, never double-reversing the original transaction.
- Crash halfway through correction leaves the prior successful head intact; duplicate correction retry adds nothing twice.
- Synthetic ADR example at a 125 mark gives realized 80, fees 2, unrealized 150, equity 100228 and net P&L 228.
