## Contracts

The [M5 shared contracts](M5-README.md#shared-contracts) and accepted ADR-010 govern this task. Proposed rules for this task:

1. Implement compare(account_id, *, snapshot, ledger_balances, internal_orders, known_at) -> ReconciliationResult according to C4. Inputs are immutable pinned records; comparison is pure.
2. Verify same account, currency/unit semantics, economic cutoff, complete source horizons and known-at bounds. Compare cash, nonzero holdings and normalized open-order client IDs/status/remaining quantities with no rounding tolerance.
3. A known delivery gap is INCOMPLETE and lists references. A supposedly complete internal prefix missing a broker execution is a discrepancy. Do not report MATCH merely because both selected subsets happen to agree.
4. Every difference records exact internal/reported values and the source record IDs. Receivables, reserved cash and buying power cannot silently replace reported cash.
5. This task produces results only; incident persistence is M5-09 and actual scheduling M5-10.

## Acceptance tests

- Exact complete match; separate seeded cash, share, open-order and missing-fill discrepancies.
- Different accounts/currency/cash basis, future snapshot, missing coverage or stale cutoff cannot pass.
- Fill known only after the snapshot cutoff does not contaminate the earlier comparison; posted-later knowledge is explicit.
- A legitimate delivery gap gives INCOMPLETE with reasons; complete-prefix missing-fill evidence gives MISMATCH.
- No comparator path calls a broker, changes OMS state or posts ledger entries.
