## Goal
M3's visible result: a CLI that replays NVDA's daily views into a research account and shows the proposed intents with their approve, reject or reduce reasons (architecture Part K; PRD §8).

## Allowed files
- `investment_engine/intents_cli.py` (new): `views replay`, `intents propose|list|show`
- `investment_engine/cli.py`: registration only
- `tests/engine/test_intents_cli.py` (new)
- `docs/tasks/M3-14.md`, `PROGRESS.md`

## Contracts
CLI:
- `views replay --instrument EQ_US_NVDA --from DATE --to DATE`: runs M2's `run_views` over the local market-data store into the lineage store. Offline, no downloads.
- `intents propose --account A (--at T | --from DATE --to DATE)`: `propose`, or `propose_over_replay` for a RESEARCH account.
- `intents list --account A [--state S] [--session DATE]` and `intents show ID [--version N]`.

## Rules
1. **Honest header on every output:** account id and kind, autonomy level and name, "PAPER MODE" or "RESEARCH ACCOUNT: SIMULATED / REPLAY", "NO ORDERS: order management arrives in M4", "KILL SWITCH: NOT BUILT (M4)", "PORTFOLIO: DECLARED STARTING STATE (no ledger until M5)", and the limits and rules versions in force. Safety state is always visible (PRD principle 2).
2. **Per intent:** id, every version with side, quantity, type, limit, TIF, reference price and bar, target session, state; each policy and risk check with its reason; reductions as "v1 80 -> v2 64: position 12.4% > 10% (risk-1)"; deadline and expiry in UTC and exchange time; approver; lineage id. For views without an intent, the ProposalRecord reason (e.g. "ABSTAIN: required data not OK").
3. **Missing never prints as zero:** unavailable snapshots, ADV or decisions show their reasons.
4. **Deterministic text** for the same database state, ASCII only (the PowerShell cp1252 baseline issue), and no DSN or other secret in any output.
5. Read-only except `views replay` and `intents propose`, which write through the M2 and M3 stores. Nothing downloads market data. Missing local data is reported with the existing `import-yfinance` command the owner would run, without running it.

## Acceptance tests
- On a synthetic store: replay views, propose over a short range for a Level 1 RESEARCH account, then list and show. The output contains each outcome (NO_INTENT, POLICY_REJECTED, RISK_REJECTED, a reduction, AWAITING_APPROVAL) with its reason, and the header labels.
- A PAPER account shows every intent POLICY_REJECTED for promotion, with the reason.
- Two runs give identical output; no `0` appears where a value is unavailable; no non-ASCII characters; no DSN text.
- `views replay` with no local data reports what's missing and writes nothing.

## Out of scope
HTML or dashboard views (M8), live data (M7), research overlays (M6).
