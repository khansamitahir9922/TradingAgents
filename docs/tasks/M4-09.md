## Goal
After the first fill, sizing, risk and the gate must see the account as it is.
- Positions and cash come from the declared start plus recorded fills, adjusted for splits and dividends (D34).
- Open orders count against both funding and the exposure limits.
- Everything is point in time.
- Old snapshots keep their ids.

This is a labelled projection, not a ledger. M5's ledger becomes authoritative (D29, D30, D34; Law 13; Part F).

## Rules
1. **Point in time** (B6, R3). The query cutoff `at` fixes everything the projection may use:
   - fills with `recorded_at ≤ at`;
   - corporate actions whose revision was known at `at` (the latest revision known then; M3 knowledge rules);
   - each order's state and filled quantity **as of `at`** (`OrderStore.as_of`), never today's status.

   An order that becomes final later still reserves at any earlier `at`. A correction or a fill learned after `at` never changes the projection at `at`.
2. **A chronological projection** (R3). The projection replays, in time order, the declared start (at its `effective_at`), each known **fill at its `executed_at`**, and each known **action at its effective time**. It never sums fills first and adjusts afterwards. The action source is the same provider as the bars (M3's `source`), and each action's revision reference is kept. Ordering rules:
   - A **SPLIT** takes effect at the **open of its ex-date session**, before any fill executed at or after that open. A fill executed on the ex-date is already in post-split shares and prices. The held quantity is multiplied by the ratio when the split is applied.
   - A **DIVIDEND's** entitlement is fixed by the shares held **at the close of the session before the ex-date**. Fills executed at or after the ex-date's open neither gain nor lose it. The amount `entitled shares × amount` becomes a **receivable**. It counts in equity, but **not** in available cash, because the pay date isn't modelled.
   - **Same time:** actions effective at a session's open come before fills executed at that open; among fills, order by `(executed_at, fill_id)`; among actions, by `(effective time, action id)`.
   - A **delayed fill**, known only after its execution, is placed at its `executed_at` in every projection whose cutoff is at or after its `recorded_at`. Earlier cutoffs never see it.
   - BUY fills add shares and subtract `quantity × price + fee`; SELL fills do the reverse. All Decimal, with whole shares.

   Example (an acceptance case): 10 shares held, a 10-for-1 split, then a BUY of 10 post-split shares gives **110**, never 200.
3. **Unsupported and uncertain cases are Unavailable, with the reason** (D34; R3):
   - no established M3 action coverage for the instrument and period: Unavailable, exactly as M3;
   - a split leaving a fraction of a share (cash in lieu is unknown): Unavailable from that date;
   - an action type other than SPLIT or DIVIDEND, or a DIVIDEND in another currency: Unavailable;
   - **an unresolved order crossing an action** (V3): an order not final as of `at`, of **any** non-final state (NEW, SUBMITTING, SUBMITTED, PARTIALLY_FILLED, CANCEL_PENDING, UNKNOWN), where the action's effective time `e` satisfies **`order.created_at < e ≤ at`**. **Equality with the target-session open counts**, because a split at the very open of the order's session crosses it. The test is made with the actions known at `at`, **whatever the gate knew** when it ran: an action learned after submission still crosses. The order's share and price basis can't be established, so the projection is **Unavailable for new proposals and sizing**, with "order X unresolved across <action>".
     - The order's terms and reservation are **never changed**: they stay in their original units and count in full (D20, D30). Nothing is silently freed.
     - The gate's `positions` input is then Missing, so other NEW orders of the account are gate-rejected, which is the safe side.
   - A **NEW** order crossing an action is covered by the same rule. In addition, the gate rejects it once the action is known (`action_since_reference`, M4-05). Its reservation stays until that GATE_REJECTED is recorded. The gate is a second guard, never the only one.
   - Each adjustment is listed in the projection with the action's revision reference.
4. **Funding and exposure** (D30; B6). Every order open at `at` (NEW, SUBMITTING, SUBMITTED, PARTIALLY_FILLED, CANCEL_PENDING, UNKNOWN) counts its unfilled remainder:
   - **Funding:** an open BUY reserves `remainder × limit + fee` from available cash; an open SELL reserves its shares from available shares.
   - **Exposure:** for the position cap (D1) and the gross-exposure check, the pending BUY remainder at its limit is **added** to the instrument's position and to gross exposure. An **unfilled SELL never reduces** holdings for any cap. Example: two separately proposed $10,000 buys under a 10% cap on $100,000: the second sees $10,000 pending and is reduced to $0, then rejected.
   - UNKNOWN orders count in full (D20).
5. **The snapshot.** The source is `PROVISIONAL`. It stores cash, receivables, positions, reservations, the adjustments, the `economic_fingerprint` (M4-02) and the hashes of the fill and order histories it used. PROVISIONAL is shown wherever cash or positions are displayed. Nothing here is called a balance.
6. **Old ids are preserved** (B6). Fields added in M4 are omitted from the canonical content when they are absent, so every M3 (declared) snapshot keeps exactly its content and `snapshot_id`. This is tested by decoding a real row written by `main`'s M3 code and checking its stored id. No retained record is reset or rewritten.
7. **Accounts without orders** get exactly M3's snapshot, sizing and risk results (regression).
8. **Valuation must match the share basis** (V3). Positions are valued at M3-07's last completed raw daily close. A close from **before** an action that has taken effect (`e ≤ at`), for an instrument held across it, is in the wrong units. So from `e` until the first raw close of a session at or after the action's ex-date is known, that instrument's valuation, and so the snapshot, is **Unavailable** ("valuation awaits a post-<action> close").
   - Old raw price × new share quantity is never computed. Example: 10 shares at a pre-split close of 1,000 become 100 shares; the snapshot is unavailable, never 100,000.
   - No snapshot is stored in that interval, so **no drawdown peak can come from it** (peaks are computed from stored snapshots only, M3-06 D3).
   - The same applies on a dividend's ex-date: the pre-ex close plus the receivable would double-count. So valuation waits for the ex-date's own close.
   - In practice the daily flow proposes after the close, when that close is known, so the interval covers the trading day itself.
   - No derived (adjusted) valuation is introduced. Reservations aren't valued as positions.
9. **Dividend timing, two separate moments** (V3): **entitlement** is measured from the shares held at the close of the session before the ex-date; the **receivable is recognized** at the ex-date's effective time, the open. Both are recorded with the action's revision reference.

## Acceptance tests
- Hand-computed cash and positions after a BUY fill and then a SELL fill, with fees.
- Point in time: a fill recorded after `at` isn't counted, and an order cancelled after `at` still reserves at `at`.
- **Exposure:** the two $10,000 buys case; a pending SELL doesn't free cap room; an UNKNOWN BUY keeps its exposure and reservation.
- **Corporate actions (R3):**
  - pre-split holdings plus a post-split fill: 10 shares, a 10-for-1 split, a BUY of 10, gives 110;
  - a BUY executed the session before the ex-date earns the dividend, and a BUY on the ex-date doesn't; a SELL on the ex-date keeps the entitlement, and a SELL the session before loses it;
  - an action correction learned after `at` leaves the projection at `at` unchanged;
  - a delayed pre-split fill, known later, is placed before the split in projections whose cutoff is after its `recorded_at`, and is absent from earlier ones;
  - an UNKNOWN pre-split BUY still open after the ex-date gives Unavailable for proposals, keeps its full reservation in original units, and changes no approved term;
  - **V3 boundary:** an UNKNOWN order whose target open **equals** the split's effective time, with the split learned only after its gate, crosses and gives Unavailable; a NEW order across that boundary does the same, and is gate-rejected once the split is known;
  - **V3 valuation:** snapshots just before the split open (valued normally), just after it (Unavailable, never tenfold equity), and after the first post-split close (valued in post-split units); the Unavailable interval stores no snapshot and creates no peak; a dividend ex-date day waits for its own close;
  - a split leaving a fraction, an unsupported action type, and missing coverage each give Unavailable, as M3 does;
  - a dividend receivable counts in equity and isn't spendable.
- The gate's `positions` check after a fill, and after a new open order, since the proposal; an unchanged portfolio at later prices passes.
- Compatibility: the captured `main` M3 snapshot row decodes, and its id matches; M3 regression tests pass unchanged.

## Out of scope
The ledger, P&L and reconciliation (M5).
