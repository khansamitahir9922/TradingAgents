## Goal
Give sizing and risk two exact, point-in-time inputs:
- the raw Decimal reference price of the view's decision bar, which exists whether or not anything is held (Codex B1);
- the account's cash, positions, value and drawdown baseline (Part F Portfolio, Part H lineage "portfolio snapshot").

## Rules
1. **Reference price** (B1).
   - `decision_reference` finds the view's decision bar among the lineage's `bar_refs`: the D1 bar of `snapshot.session_date` from the view's own source and instrument. It reads **exactly that revision** through `knowledge` (via the store's revision history).
   - It returns `Unavailable` with a reason when:
     - the bar is missing, or isn't available and delivered by `at`;
     - the lineage names more than one candidate, or none;
     - the source, instrument or revision differs from the lineage;
     - the bar isn't CLOSED;
     - the candle's currency differs from the instrument's.
   - `close` is the raw Decimal from the candle. M2's split-adjusted float `decision_close` is never used and is refused if offered, by type check.
   - A missing holding means zero held, never a missing price.
2. **Declared, and labelled so.** In M3 the only source is the declared starting state (default: $100,000 cash, no positions, D14). Every display says DECLARED, not ledger-derived; the ledger replaces it in M5. Declaring a start is Admin-only and audited, allowed once per account in M3 (a second declaration raises). For RESEARCH accounts it takes `--effective-at` (replay time); PAPER accounts use the live time. Authorization uses the real command time, and `starting_state_ref` hashes the content without `recorded_at` (M3-05 rule 5, re-review R2/S2).
3. **Positions at the expected session, never an older bar** (S2). A held position is valued at the raw close of the bar of the **latest completed regular session at or before `as_of`**, according to the calendar, read through `knowledge`. If that session's bar is missing or not yet known, the snapshot is `Unavailable` ("bar for <session> not known at <as_of>"). An older CLOSED bar is never substituted. With no positions, no bar is needed.
4. **Corporate actions.** Quantities are as of `effective_at`. If verified coverage shows a split or dividend between `effective_at` and `as_of` for a held instrument, the snapshot is unavailable ("action since the declared start; ledger needed (M5)"). Missing coverage is also unavailable. Holding no positions needs no action coverage.
5. **Drawdown baseline** (S6). `baseline_at` is the declared start or, if later, the latest halt clear at or before `as_of`. `peak_equity` is the maximum equity of stored snapshots in [`baseline_at`, `as_of`], plus this one. After a clear, the clearing equity (M3-06 `baseline_equity`) is included as a floor for the peak.
6. **Decimal throughout** (Law 16): quantity × price in Decimal, with a fresh context per calculation. No floats. Snapshots are append-only; ids hash the content.
7. Only D1 views are used in M3 (D12).

## Acceptance tests
- **First BUY input from an empty account:** the default start plus a BULLISH view gives a RawReference equal to the decision bar's raw close and a snapshot with no positions; `held("EQ_US_NVDA") == 0`.
- **Reference refusals:**
  - wrong provider, wrong instrument or a different revision in the store;
  - a correction available before T but delivered after T (the original is used, or Unavailable when the lineage names the correction);
  - equal-time sequence ordering;
  - a FORMING bar, a currency mismatch, a missing bar;
  - a float or adjusted value passed in.
- **Held position:** a declared position is valued at the expected session's raw close. A missing expected-session bar is Unavailable even when an older bar exists. A split after the start and missing coverage are Unavailable.
- **Peak and baseline:** a hand-calculated drawdown over a rising-then-falling series; after a halt clear, the peak resets to the clearing equity.
- The same inputs give the same id; a second or non-Admin starting declaration is refused and writes nothing.

## Out of scope
Ledger-derived positions and P&L (M5), fees, multi-currency, fractional shares, live quotes (M7).
