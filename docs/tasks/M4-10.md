## Goal
Replace M3's fixed "PROMOTION NOT ASSESSED" with an honest assessment. It is computed from one pinned, stored walk-forward evaluation record, using defined aggregate formulas, against versioned, Admin-published thresholds. Evidence is never attributed to the wrong recipe, and PAPER stays blocked without shadow evidence (Law 19; Part K; PRD UC-11; D21, D22).

## Rules
1. **The evidence target and the recipe identity** (B9, R4). An assessment is of **one `EvaluationRecord`, by `record_id`**. The decision recipe isn't in `evaluation_config`, which holds only `version`, `initial_cash`, `currency`, `costs_bps_per_side`, `exposure` and `dividend_treatment`. So the assessment **derives the recipe from decision lineage**:
   1. For every fold's `EvaluationRun`, resolve each of its `lineage_refs` through the existing `LineageStore.get`.
   2. From each `LineageRecord`, take the **recipe fields**: `policy_version`, `strategy_versions`, `feature_versions`, `config` (the validated policy and feature parameters) and `health_thresholds`. Normalize them in canonical form and hash them: the **recipe identity** (`rcp-` + SHA-256). Session-specific fields (`generated_at`, `bar_refs`, `action_refs`, `snapshot`, `signals`, `decision`, `data_health`) are **never** part of it, so a later view of the same recipe has the same identity.
   3. **Refuse** (REFUSED, with reasons) when: any referenced lineage is missing; a run has no lineage refs; the records give **more than one recipe identity** (mixed recipes); or a record's `instrument_id` or provider (`snapshot.source`) differs from the evaluation's instrument and source.

   The **evidence context** is kept separately and pinned: `record_id`, source, instrument, `evaluation_config` (costs and exposure), the walk-forward configuration, and code and calendar versions. It isn't part of the recipe identity.

   **Matching a PAPER intent's view** (the `promotion` check): its view's lineage gives a recipe identity by the same derivation. A view matches an assessment when the **recipe identity, source and instrument** are equal. A different recipe is refused, whatever its execution settings. Code-version differences are shown, not matched. Existing M2 evaluation records and their ids are unchanged: nothing is added to them retroactively, and no recipe metadata is invented.
2. **Eligibility.** The record must be complete: every fold is complete, with no stitched value unavailable. Its cost model must be at least as costly as the thresholds' minimum (D21: 5 bps per side). An ineligible record is **refused**, with reasons, and is never PARTIAL.
3. **The formulas** (B9):
   - **Out-of-sample daily return series:** each test fold's daily returns, from its own `equity_curve` (initial cash included, as in M2-10 B4), concatenated in fold order. Each fold starts from fresh cash, so the returns are relative and are chained, never summed as dollars.
   - **Sharpe:** the mean of that series over its sample standard deviation, times √252, with a zero risk-free rate. This is M2's daily convention, never computed from six-month fold returns.
   - **Maximum drawdown:** on the equity chained from that series, against buy-and-hold chained the same way from each fold's `buy_and_hold` benchmark `equity_curve` over the same sessions. It passes when the strategy's is no worse.
   - **Trades:** the total number of simulated executions (entries and exits, M2-10's `trades`) **across all** out-of-sample folds, not per fold.
   - **Positive folds:** the share of folds with net return > 0.
4. **The record kept** has: the assessment id; the evaluation `record_id` and recipe identity; the thresholds version; `as_of` (when it was assessed); each criterion's value, threshold and pass or fail; and the overall PASSED, FAILED or REFUSED. It is deterministic: the same inputs give the same id.
5. **A record, not a permission** (Law 19). PASSED satisfies D21 only. A PAPER account also needs D22's shadow evidence, whose comparison band is **not yet specified** (left open, not implied by "3 months"). With no shadow mode in M4, the PAPER `promotion` check fails and says which part is missing. RESEARCH accounts are unaffected (D23).
6. **No prediction.** Documents and output never state an outcome before it is computed.

## Acceptance tests
- **Hand-computed, multi-fold:** three folds with known daily curves. The daily-series Sharpe differs from a fold-return Sharpe, and the test pins the daily one. Chained drawdown is checked against buy-and-hold. The trade total across folds passes at 30 while each fold alone has fewer than 30.
- Each criterion failing alone; an incomplete fold refuses; costs below the minimum refuse.
- **Attribution (R4):**
  - two stored evaluations with identical cost and execution configs but different recipe parameters (`config`) give different recipe identities;
  - a missing lineage record refuses;
  - a run mixing two recipes refuses, and evidence mixing two providers refuses;
  - a later view of the same recipe, with a different time and data, matches, while an unrelated recipe is refused;
  - the existing M2 record's id is unchanged.
- PAPER with a PASSED assessment still fails `promotion` ("shadow evidence missing"); RESEARCH passes.
- Publishing a new thresholds version leaves earlier assessments unchanged.

## Out of scope
Shadow mode (live data, M7), the D22 band and Level 2 criteria (not set).
