## Goal
Produce labelled SIMULATED research performance at next-bar prices with stated costs, and matched buy-and-hold/cash benchmarks. This is initial evaluation, not an OMS or execution replay.

@dataclass(frozen=True)
class EvaluationConfig:
    version: str; initial_cash: Decimal; currency: str
    costs_bps_per_side: Decimal          # provisional 5; stress run 10
    exposure: str                       # LONG_OR_CASH in the first research experiment
    dividend_treatment: str             # CASH_ON_EX_DATE_RESEARCH_ASSUMPTION

@dataclass(frozen=True)

    decision_lineage_id: str; decision_time: datetime; simulated_time: datetime

    lineage_refs: tuple[str, ...]; input_refs: tuple[BarRef | ActionRef, ...]
    limitations: tuple[str, ...]

def simulate_research(views, *, reader, calendar, source, instrument_id,

## Rules
1. Views come from replay using each historical decision T. Later revisions may describe labelled realised outcomes at report `at`, but can never feed earlier decisions. Both decision and realised-outcome revision references are retained. A future open cannot be used to create the view that chooses its trade.
2. A decision can simulate at the next regular-session open strictly after `generated_at`, and only if still valid at that open. At equal timestamps a decision at an open cannot trade at that same open. BULLISH targets long; BEARISH/NEUTRAL targets cash. ABSTAIN schedules no new trade and preserves existing exposure in this proposed experiment, with counts and an explicit label; missing execution data makes the run incomplete, not zero-priced. Repeated unchanged exposure causes no trade. Latest valid decision wins if several target the same open.
3. Use raw as-traded prices for simulated transactions and quantities. On a split, multiply shares before ex-date trading; don't create a fictitious loss or turnover. Model cash dividends on ex-date for research, clearly disclosing this differs from payment-date broker cash/ledger accounting. Dividend and split coverage must be established; no action is assumed absent merely from an empty query.
4. Initial portfolio is cash only; buys use affordable quantities rounded down to SHARE_QUANTUM, with proportional costs included. Residual cash is retained. Prices/quantities/cash/costs stay Decimal; derived return metrics cross the approved float64 boundary. Costs are a combined research assumption per side, not a separately duplicated fee and slippage charge. Fractional-share affordability is a simulation assumption, not a broker claim.
5. Buy-and-hold buys at the same first eligible open, with the same cash, costs, split/dividend treatment, dates and valuation data. Cash uses explicitly assumed zero interest. Report ending marked equity without forced liquidation and disclose remaining exposure. Report net return, maximum drawdown and daily-return Sharpe (zero risk-free assumption, 252-session annualization); zero variance/insufficient history produces unavailable Sharpe. Count entries/round trips explicitly rather than calling every daily observation a trade.
6. Store EvaluationRun in the Operational Database's evaluation domain, append-only, via M2-11. No broker port, OrderIntent, Order, ledger, outbox, permissions or new process is added. Real paper fills later remain recorded as reported.

## Implementation choices
Resolved: the complete formulas above make this draft testable. All monetary values are research-simulation values, never shown as actual ledger cash. No real data download needed for implementation tests.

Resolved for M2 (owner, 2026-10-14): the exposure, ABSTAIN, dividend and cost assumptions above (5 bps per side, 10 bps stress) are accepted as **provisional research assumptions**, versioned in `EvaluationConfig` and disclosed in the report. Changing them reruns the evaluation. The proposed five-session direction horizon does not imply a five-session holding rule; exposure follows subsequent views as specified here.

## Acceptance tests
- Next-open, same-open exclusion, expiry, conflicting queued decisions, repeated exposure and ABSTAIN behaviour on a small synthetic calendar.
- Exact cash/quantity/cost arithmetic for 5 and 10 bps; cash remains non-negative after rounding. Synthetic split and dividend preserve appropriate economic value.
- Buy-and-hold and strategy share dates/costs/actions; missing execution bar makes results incomplete and identified.
- Known equity sequence gives hand-calculated net return/drawdown/Sharpe; zero-variance Sharpe unavailable.
- Adding a late correction changes labelled realised-outcome revisions but not earlier decision lineage; no broker/OMS/ledger writes or clock reads.

## Out of scope
Execution replay and crash recovery (M4), real paper fills (M7), shorts, leverage, taxes, financing and promotion authority.
